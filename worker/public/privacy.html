Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data AutoChat 365 ("we", "us") collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It covers two different groups of people: businesses who sign up to build a chatbot with us ("account holders" or "tenants"), and people who chat with a widget an account holder has embedded on their own website ("visitors").

Who we are

AutoChat 365 is operated by [legal entity name], [registered address]. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@autochat365.com.

If you're an account holder

We collect your email address when you sign up, and use it to send you a sign-in link and essential service communications. We store the settings you configure for your widget(s) (branding, behaviour, allowed websites) and the documents you upload to your knowledge base.

We process this data to provide the service you've asked for, which is our lawful basis under UK GDPR (Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).

If you're a visitor chatting with a widget

When you use a chat widget embedded on a business's website, we process:

  • The messages you send, and the assistant's replies, so the conversation can continue and so the business can review it later;
  • A session identifier stored in your browser (via localStorage) so the chat can resume if you close and reopen the widget — this is used only to keep your conversation working, not for advertising or tracking across other websites;
  • The website origin the widget was loaded from and your browser's user-agent string, for security and abuse prevention.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, passwords, or similar credentials through chat — our systems automatically detect and remove these from messages before they're stored, where technically possible. Please still avoid sharing sensitive information you don't need to.

The business whose widget you're chatting with is the controller of your conversation for most practical purposes: they decide what the assistant is used for, and they can access transcripts of chats with their own widget. We act as their processor, storing and handling the data on their behalf and providing the underlying AI-powered chat service. If you have a question about how a specific business uses your conversation, you may need to contact that business directly, as well as us.

Our lawful basis for this processing is legitimate interests — both ours and the business's — in providing a functioning support/chat service that you have chosen to use.

How we generate replies

To answer a message, we send it (together with relevant parts of the business's own knowledge base) to third-party AI language-model providers, which generate the reply text and the embeddings used to find relevant content. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we require appropriate safeguards to be in place before we rely on a given provider for this processing. We're currently reviewing which providers we use and how those safeguards are documented, and we'll update this section with specifics once that review is complete.

How long we keep data

We keep account and conversation data for as long as your account (or the relevant widget) is active, so the service keeps working as expected. We don't currently have an automated deletion schedule; if you'd like data deleted sooner, contact us and we'll action the request (see "Your rights" below).

Who we share data with

We share data with the sub-processors who help us run the service, including:

  • Supabase, for database hosting and authentication;
  • Cloudflare, for hosting our application and handling web traffic;
  • Third-party AI providers, as described above, to generate chat replies and embeddings.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it (right of access);
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data (right to rectification);
  • Ask us to delete your data, subject to certain exceptions, e.g. where we need to keep it for legal reasons (right to erasure);
  • Ask us to export your data in a portable format (right to data portability);
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (right to object).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@autochat365.com. We don't yet have a self-service tool for this, so we'll action requests manually; we aim to respond within one month, as required by law.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if you believe we've handled your data unlawfully.

Security

Each business's data is kept logically separated from every other business's using database-level access controls, and every request is checked against them. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we treat security as an ongoing responsibility, not a one-time task.

Children

Our service is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
