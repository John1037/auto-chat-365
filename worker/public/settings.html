Settings

Data retention

Applies to every widget on this account. Once a conversation reaches this age, its messages and the conversation itself are permanently deleted -- aggregate daily statistics (conversation/message counts, etc.) are kept regardless, so past performance stays visible after the raw transcripts age out.

Danger zone

Deleting your account is permanent. All conversations, messages, documents, widgets, and members associated with this account will be unrecoverably deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.
